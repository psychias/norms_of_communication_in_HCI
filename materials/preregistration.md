# Pre-registration

The study design, hypotheses (H1-H4), primary dependent variables (acceptability, justifiability, reliance),
sample-size rationale, exclusion rule and confirmatory analysis plan were pre-registered on AsPredicted
before data collection.

The registration is hosted by AsPredicted and is not redistributed here; the link above opens the permanent
record. Data collection on Prolific took place on 19 March 2026.

## Confirmatory versus exploratory

- **Confirmatory (pre-registered):** 2 (Stake: high vs. low) x 3 (Agent: Human-High-Skin, Human-Low-Skin, AI)
  between-subjects ANOVAs on the three primary outcomes, Bonferroni-corrected pairwise contrasts among the
  agent levels, and independent-samples t-tests for the manipulation checks. Exclusion rule: failure of the
  first attention check.
- **Exploratory (not pre-registered):** all analyses of the secondary outcomes (DV4-DV7), the covariate,
  equivalence (TOST), Bayesian, Tobit, Holm-Bonferroni, reliability and qualitative analyses. These are the
  analyses reproduced by `code/supplementary_analyses.py` and are labelled exploratory in the manuscript.
